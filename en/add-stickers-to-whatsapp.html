How to add stickers to WhatsApp

Download a single sticker as WebP and use it in WhatsApp’s sticker maker, or add a whole pack by opening the .wastickers file with a sticker importer app on Android.

Adding a single sticker

Recent WhatsApp versions have an option to create your own sticker in the chat’s sticker section. You can pick the WebP file you downloaded from viveSticker there as an image. Menu names vary by version and device.

  1. 1 Edit your sticker in viveSticker and tap “Download WebP”.
  2. 2 In WhatsApp, open a chat, go to the sticker section and choose the create option.
  3. 3 Pick the downloaded file and send the sticker; star it in the chat to add it to your favourites.

Adding a pack (Android)

WhatsApp doesn’t allow packs to be added straight from a browser. Packs are added through an app that uses WhatsApp’s official sticker interface.

  1. 1 Build your pack in viveSticker and download the .wastickers file.
  2. 2 Install a sticker importer app from Google Play that opens .wastickers files.
  3. 3 Open the downloaded file with that app and confirm “Add to WhatsApp”.

On iPhone

On iOS the choice of apps that open .wastickers packs is limited. The most reliable way on iPhone is to add stickers one by one with WhatsApp’s sticker maker.

Frequently asked

Why is there no direct “Add to WhatsApp” button?

WhatsApp only lets packs be added through apps on the device. Websites can’t reach that interface.

My sticker shows up as a photo in the chat. Why?

If the file was sent as an image, WhatsApp treats it as a photo. Use the sticker maker or a pack instead.

The pack I added doesn’t appear. What now?

Fully close and reopen WhatsApp. If the pack breaks a rule (such as fewer than 3 stickers) the importer app shows an error; viveSticker checks these before you download.
